Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you get started.

Do I approve every post?

Yes. You review the content and can edit, replace, approve, or skip anything before posting.

Does AI HVAC Marketing post to my accounts?

No. You receive a customized, branded content package. Your company keeps control of its own social accounts and decides when to publish.

Will the system invent discounts or promotions?

No. Offer posts use only pricing, promotions, or offers supplied and approved by your company.

Which platforms are included?

Each post includes versions for Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business Profile.

What is the difference between the 4-post and 8-post plans?

The full calendar contains eight posts. Four-post clients receive an alternating selection from that same educational-and-offer schedule.
